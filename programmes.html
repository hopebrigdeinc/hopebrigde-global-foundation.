What we focus on

Programmes shaped around human needs.

Our programme framework sets out the areas in which the foundation intends to contribute. Each project should be announced with confirmed details and transparent reporting.

01

Humanitarian assistance

Relief and practical support for vulnerable people facing hardship, crisis or displacement.

02

Education

Educational access, learning materials, scholarships and opportunities for children and young people, subject to available resources and programme approval.

03

Healthcare

Community health education, outreach and support initiatives delivered with qualified providers where required.

04

Poverty alleviation

Initiatives that strengthen household resilience, access to opportunity and pathways towards economic stability.

05

Food and shelter

Food assistance and support for safe, dignified living conditions for people experiencing vulnerability.

06

Youth empowerment

Skills, mentoring and participation opportunities that help young people shape their futures.

07

Disaster relief

Preparedness, emergency response and recovery support, coordinated with relevant local actors.

These are programme areas and intended activities. They should not be read as confirmation that a specific project has been completed or is currently accepting applications.
