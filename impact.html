Our achievements

Impact that reaches across borders.

Hopebrigde Global Foundation's reported achievements reflect the reach of its humanitarian activities and commitment to underserved communities.

40+Countries impacted worldwide
200,000+Children supported through scholarships and educational assistance
500,000+Individuals reached through food assistance programmes
150,000+People reached through free medical outreach services
25,000+Families assisted during humanitarian emergencies

One million lives and counting.

The foundation reports that its work has impacted more than one million lives individually through humanitarian assistance, education, healthcare, food support, water initiatives and community development.
