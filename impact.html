Transparency & reporting

Impact should be measured, evidenced and shared.

We are committed to reporting outcomes responsibly and making the difference made by each project understandable.

Our impact reporting approach

The following figures were supplied for the website brief. They have not been independently verified in this build and are therefore not presented as established foundation achievements.

Verification required: Before publishing these as achievements, confirm source records, reporting period, definitions, geographic scope, deduplication method and supporting evidence. Until then, do not use them in fundraising or grant representations as verified results.

Figures proposed for verification

  • Over 40 countries impacted worldwide.
  • 200,000+ children supported through scholarships and educational assistance worldwide.
  • 500,000+ individuals reached through food assistance programmes.
  • 150,000+ people provided with access to free medical outreach services.
  • 25,000+ families assisted during humanitarian emergencies.
  • 200+ community water projects established in underserved locations, including over 150 in Africa.
  • 1 million+ lives impacted individually.

What a project report should include

  • Project name, location and delivery dates.
  • Objectives, target population and delivery partners.
  • Number of unique people reached and how it was counted.
  • Budget, funding source and expenditure summary where appropriate.
  • Evidence, safeguarding measures, lessons learned and follow-up plans.

As verified reports become available, this page can be updated with dated project summaries, photographs used with consent and measurable outcomes.
