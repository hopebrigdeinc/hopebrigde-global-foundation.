International Grant Programme

Expanding opportunity and supporting individuals and families facing financial hardship.

About the programme

The Hopebrigde Global Foundation International Grant Programme is a humanitarian initiative being developed to provide financial assistance and support to eligible individuals and families experiencing financial hardship across different countries.

The programme aims to promote economic stability, improve living conditions, support educational opportunities and empower communities through financial grants and humanitarian assistance.

Programme objectives

  • Provide support to individuals and families experiencing financial hardship.
  • Promote economic stability and improved living conditions.
  • Encourage educational opportunities and personal development.
  • Support community resilience and inclusive development.

Proposed eligibility framework

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years old.
  • Applicants should be individuals experiencing genuine financial hardship.
  • Applicants must reside in a country covered by the programme.
  • Previous beneficiaries may be excluded from subsequent batches.
  • Successful applicants will receive an official award notification.
  • Grant amounts and disbursement arrangements will be clearly stated in each official programme announcement.

Programme announcements and applications

Programme announcements and application information are anticipated in 2026. Details will be published through the foundation's official communication channels when programme arrangements, application periods, participating countries and application processes are confirmed.

Each announcement will explain the application requirements, assessment process, relevant dates and how applicants can receive official updates.
