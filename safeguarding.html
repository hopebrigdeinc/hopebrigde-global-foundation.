Policies

Safeguarding commitment

Respect, safety and dignity must guide all work involving children and adults at risk.

This is a policy starter, not a complete safeguarding system. The foundation should appoint a trained safeguarding lead, adopt reporting procedures and obtain jurisdiction-specific review before activities begin.

Our commitment

Hopebrigde Global Foundation recognises the importance of protecting children and adults at risk from abuse, exploitation, neglect and harm. Anyone involved in activities should be treated with dignity and respect.

Expected conduct

  • Put the safety and best interests of children and adults at risk first.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries and avoid unsupervised one-to-one situations where prohibited by policy.
  • Obtain informed consent for photographs, interviews and personal stories.
  • Never exchange assistance for photographs, publicity or personal favours.
  • Report safeguarding concerns promptly through an approved reporting channel.
  • Protect confidential information and share it only when necessary and lawful.

Reporting concerns

The foundation is committed to promoting safe, respectful and inclusive environments across its activities. Safeguarding concerns may be raised through the foundation's official contact channel.
